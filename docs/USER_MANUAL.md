# 📘 Campus Management System — Comprehensive User Manual

Complete institutional end-user manual covering operations for **System Administrators**, **Faculty Members**, **Students**, **Department Heads (HODs)**, **Bursar/Finance Officers**, **Librarians**, and **Training & Placement Officers (TPO)**.

---

## 1. Role-Based Navigation Overview

| Role | Primary Portal Access |
|---|---|
| 👑 **Administrator** | Executive Dashboard, Students 360, Faculty Directory, Departments, Fee Realization, System Audit Reports |
| 👨‍🏫 **Faculty Member** | Faculty Dashboard, Roll-Call Attendance, Course Catalog & Syllabus, Timetable Matrix, Marks Entry, Assignment Grading |
| 🎓 **Student** | Student Dashboard, Attendance Compliance, Official Grade Card, Assignment Submissions, Fee Invoices & Receipts, Library Checkouts, Placement Drives |
| 🏛️ **Head of Department (HOD)** | Department Workload, Exam Results Verification, Curriculum Review, Timetable Conflict Auditing |
| 💳 **Bursar / Finance Officer** | Fee Structures Setup, Payment Processing, Invoice Ledger, Printable Receipts, Financial Audit CSV Exports |
| 📚 **Librarian** | Book Cataloging, Circulation Desk, Book Issue/Return, Automated Overdue Fine Calculations |
| 💼 **Placement Officer (TPO)** | Corporate Hiring Drives, Package Tracking (CTC LPA), Student Application Shortlisting, Offer Letters |

## 2. Step-by-Step Workflows

### 🏫 Workflow A: Faculty Attendance & Roll Call
1. Navigate to **Attendance** in the left sidebar.
2. Select **Subject** (e.g. `CSE-101: Data Structures`), **Class Section** (`Year 2 Sec A`), and **Session Date**.
3. Toggle student statuses (`Present`, `Absent`, `Late`, `Leave`).
4. Click **Save Attendance**. Metrics immediately reflect in student dossiers and condonation shortage audits.

---

### 📝 Workflow B: Examination Marks & Official Grade Card
1. Admin creates an Examination (e.g. `Fall 2026 Mid-Terms`).
2. Faculty enters Internal & External marks in **Examinations & Grades**.
3. System automatically computes Letter Grades (`A+`, `A`, `B+`, etc.) and Grade Points based on the 10-point standard engine.
4. HOD reviews and clicks **Verify & Publish Results**.
5. Students view and download their verified **Official Grade Card** with credit-weighted SGPA.

---

### 💵 Workflow C: Fee Payment & Receipt Generation
1. Bursar sets up semester fee structures under **Fees & Finance**.
2. Student / Bursar selects open account and clicks **Record Payment**.
3. Selects payment method (Online Gateway, Card, Net Banking, Cash) and enters reference ID.
4. System immediately issues a digitally certified **Official Payment Receipt** with unique invoice number, tax ID, and bursar signature stamp.
5. Click **Print / Download PDF** for institutional physical records.

---

### 💼 Workflow D: Campus Placement Drive & Job Application
1. TPO schedules a new hiring drive (e.g. `Google Cloud - $24.5 LPA`).
2. Eligible students meeting the GPA cutoff view the drive under **Placements**.
3. Student clicks **Apply Now**, uploads resume/portfolio link, and submits application.
4. TPO updates interview progression stages (`Screening`, `Shortlisted`, `Technical Rounds`, `Offered`).
